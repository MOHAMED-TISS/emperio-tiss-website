# Catalogue Market Priority

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-09

The multilingual catalogue uses the Spanish catalogue as the canonical product/specification source and changes only the merchandising order and language presentation by market.

## Market logic

### Spain / ES
Mediterranean and Spanish wholesale relevance leads the catalogue: gilthead seabream, seabass, hake, monkfish and other Mediterranean references are presented before broader international references.

### United Kingdom / EN
The priority emphasises salmon, tuna, whitefish/hake and warm-water prawns. Seafish's 2025 retail data places salmon, tuna, cod, pollock and warm-water prawns among the leading species, while premium farmed species such as salmon, prawns and seabass continue to perform strongly.

### France / FR
The priority leads with salmon, cod/hake and high-value fresh references, followed by Mediterranean and shellfish references. EUMOFA's 2025 EU Fish Market reports salmon as France's leading fresh species by household volume/value, with cod stable and trout recovering.

### Italy / IT
The priority emphasises gilthead seabream, mussels, European seabass, salmon, swordfish and cephalopods. EUMOFA reports gilthead seabream as Italy's most consumed fresh species in 2024, mussels second, while seabass, salmon, octopus and squid showed resilience or growth.

### Gulf / Middle East / AR
The priority favours fish first, followed by shrimp and commercially relevant cephalopods/specialties among the products actually available in the canonical catalogue. Recent GCC market research indicates fish remains dominant, fresh/chilled formats remain important, shrimp is a growth category, and premium/fresh species such as hamour and salmon have strong positioning.

## Important rule

Market priority is **merchandising, not product invention**. A market research report may identify a product as important, but the website will not add that product unless it exists in the canonical EMPERIO TISS catalogue and has a valid product ID/specification.

## Sources reviewed

- EUMOFA, *The EU Fish Market 2025* — France and Italy consumption profiles.
- Seafish, *Total Seafood in Multiple Retail 2025* and *Farmed Seafood in Multiple Retail 2025* — UK species performance.
- Seafish, *UK Seafood Trade 2025* — import and demand trends.
- GCC/Middle East seafood market analyses updated in 2025/2026 — fish dominance, shrimp growth, fresh/chilled preference and regional premium species.

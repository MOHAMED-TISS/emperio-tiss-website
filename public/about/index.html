Our approach

From source to destination.

Our activity is built around understanding where products come from, where demand is strongest and how goods can move efficiently between markets.

EMPERIO TISS operates in professional food trade, with a focus on seafood, fish, fruits and vegetables.

We work across sourcing, commercial development, distribution and logistics, connecting producers, suppliers and professional buyers through practical market routes.

Europe and Africa are central to our sourcing and distribution activity, while our network also extends towards the Middle East.

The objective is simple: build reliable commercial relationships around products, markets and destinations that make sense.

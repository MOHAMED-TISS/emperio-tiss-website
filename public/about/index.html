Our approach

From source
to destination.

We focus on understanding products, origins, professional requirements and destination markets, then building practical supply routes around them.

EMPERIO TISS works across seafood, fish, fruits and vegetables, connecting relevant origins with professional buyers and destinations.

Our activity combines sourcing, product selection, distribution and logistics around the requirements of each market.

Europe and Africa are central to our network, with Spain, France, Italy, Germany and the Netherlands connected to origins across Morocco, Tunisia, Mauritania and West Africa.

Our network also extends towards selected Middle Eastern markets as new routes and opportunities develop.

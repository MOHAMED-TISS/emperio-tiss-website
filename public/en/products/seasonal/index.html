04 / THE FOUR SEASONS

Each season
has its produce.

A reading of the year through fruit and vegetables: the broad rhythms that guide buying.

01Winter

The time of citrus.

Clementines, mandarins and oranges define the winter. Broccoli and cauliflower are in their months of greatest availability, and tomatoes from Morocco take over at the height of their campaign.

Clementine · Mandarin · Orange · Lemon · Grapefruit

02Spring

Berries open the year.

Strawberries, blueberries and raspberries herald the spring; the first cherries and apricots follow. Green beans from Spain keep their high availability until summer.

Strawberry · Blueberry · Raspberry · Cherry · Apricot

03Summer

Stone fruit, in its fullness.

Peach, nectarine, flat peach and plum, with melon and watermelon. In Spain, tomatoes and potatoes reach their high level of availability, and garlic concentrates its campaign.

Peach · Nectarine · Flat peach · Plum · Melon · Watermelon

04Autumn

Grapes, pomegranates, figs and persimmons.

Autumn brings seasonal Mediterranean fruit and dates. For vegetables it is a time of transition: cucumbers, courgettes and aubergines from Spain return to their high level while Morocco begins its campaign.

Table grapes · Pomegranate · Fig · Persimmon · Dates

Seafood

Fresh fish follows the rhythm of the catch. Shellfish frozen at sea and frozen cephalopods allow planning with more leeway throughout the year.

Tropical fruit

Mango, pineapple, avocado, banana and the other tropical fruits are scheduled according to each product’s origin and campaign.
